Aikido Training in Gdynia

At Sesshinkan Dojo Gdynia training has a clear rhythm: a fixed schedule, a calm entry for beginners and readable rules of work on the mat. Here you will find the key information about the venue, class hours and your first contact with the dojo.

📅 Schedule

Monday 8:00 PM - 9:30 PM
Friday 8:00 PM - 9:30 PM

Evening hours work well for people who work or study.

📍 Venue

ACS - Academic Sports Centre of the Polish Naval Academy
ul. Komandora Podporucznika Jana Grudzińskiego 1
81-103 Gdynia

Easy to reach from Gdynia, Sopot and Gdańsk.

🥋 Who it is for

For beginners, people returning to movement after a break, and practitioners looking for a traditional dojo without competitive pressure.

🎁 Start

The first class is free. To begin, comfortable sportswear is enough.

What do Aikido classes in Gdynia look like?

Our Aikido classes in Gdynia combine traditional methodology with a practical, calm introduction. Training includes a warm-up, learning how to move, ukemi, basic techniques and paired work. This is not a hall oriented around sparring or sports rivalry. The priorities are technique, safety, regularity and development.

1

Arrive early

Ideally 10-15 minutes before class. That is enough to calmly walk in, change and learn the dojo rules.

2

Train from zero

Beginners do not need to know anything. The first class is an introduction, not a fitness or knowledge test.

3

Decide after class

After training you can ask about the further plan, fees and next steps - with no pressure and no obligations.

Who are the classes for?

✅ For beginners

If you have never trained a martial art before, you can start from zero.

✅ For adults

The evening schedule and the calm pace of entry suit working people well.

✅ For people looking for self-defence without the sports framing

Aikido develops control, timing, balance and work under pressure, without competitions or a sparring-based training model.

✅ For people who want to train long-term

This is not a quick-fix programme. It is a practice you can develop for years.
