Aikido in Gdynia

Evening Aikido in Gdynia for beginners and experienced practitioners, in Shihan Fumio Toyoda's lineage. A gentle introduction and your first class free. The new season is a good time to start. You can join throughout the year.

Come to your first training Schedule & directions

First training: Mon & Fri, 8:00–9:30 PM

Location: ACS — Academic Sports Center of the Polish Naval Academy, ul. Komandora Podporucznika Jana Grudzińskiego 1, Gdynia. Your first class is free, no advance sign-up needed — just arrive a few minutes early.

Who trains and what it looks like

Adults and teenagers from 12–13 train with us, including people with no sports background at all. Classes run in the evening, after work and school. For your first training a comfortable T-shirt and track pants without hard elements are enough — you practice in pairs, guided by the instructor. Student grades run 7 kyu: everyone trains in a white belt, and the hakama comes at 2 kyu. Details on the first training page.

Instructor and school

Led by Sensei Oskar Szrajer (5 dan). The Polish Aikido Organization continues the tradition of Shihan Fumio Toyoda (1947–2001) through our teacher Shihan Edward Germanov (7 dan), directly connected to Aikikai Hombu Dojo in Tokyo. More about our teacher: Sensei Oskar Szrajer and Sesshinkan Dojo.

Top questions before your first training

  • Can I start with no experience? Yes — most people start from zero.
  • How much is the first class? Your first class is free.
  • What should I bring? Track pants without hard elements, a T-shirt, water and a towel.
